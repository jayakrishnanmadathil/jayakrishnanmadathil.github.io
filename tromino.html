First choose the missing square. Then drag an L‑tromino from the pile onto the board. The subdivision guide is optional and starts hidden. Reveal one level at a time if you want to see the recursive 4-way structure.

Controls

Drag the piece to place it. You can also click the pile once and then click the board as a keyboard-friendly alternative. Rotate with the buttons or press R. Press Z to undo. Illegal placements are rejected.
